About — Iaia Colella

I work where neuroscience meets the start line.

I'm Iaia Colella, mental performance coach. My background is psychology and cognitive neuroscience, with specialisations in Agonistic Trance, Sport Coaching and NLP.

I work with national and international athletes across multiple sports. The 8-Dimension Method is the system I use with every one of them — neuroscience-grounded, performance-tested, and ruthlessly practical.

What I offer isn't theory. It's a practical system I've tested personally and proven with over 50 athletes across 10+ sports.
