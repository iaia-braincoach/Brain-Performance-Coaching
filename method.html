Common questions

How the method works.

Q · 01
How is this different from sport psychology?
Sport psychology is largely diagnostic and conversational. The 8-Dimension Method is a training system. Each session prescribes drills you practise daily — not just tools you talk about.
Q · 02
Do I have to be elite to work with you?
No. The principles work for any committed athlete. The Elite 1:1 Programme is built for national and international competitors; workshops and group cycles serve athletes earlier in the journey.
Q · 03
How long until I notice change?
Most athletes report a measurable shift in 4–6 weeks: clearer focus, calmer pre-competition state, faster recovery from mistakes. Performance gains follow once the mental groundwork is in.
Q · 04
Can you work with my coach and team?
Yes — and I prefer to. The best results come when mental training is integrated with physical training. Custom Partnerships are designed exactly for that.
Q · 05
Is the coaching in person or remote?
Both. 1:1 sessions are typically remote. Workshops and squad work are run on-site. The tools transfer cleanly between formats.
